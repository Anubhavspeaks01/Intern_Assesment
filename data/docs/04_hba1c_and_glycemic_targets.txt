# Document Title: Diagnostic Criteria, Glycemic Targets, and Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c) Monitoring
# Source: National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK) & ADA Guidelines
# Document ID: DOC-04-HBA1C-TARGETS

1. DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA FOR DIABETES AND PREDIABETES
Diabetes can be formally diagnosed using one of four validated laboratory measurements:
- Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c):
  * Normal: Below 5.7% (below 39 mmol/mol)
  * Prediabetes: 5.7% to 6.4% (39 to 47 mmol/mol)
  * Diabetes: 6.5% or higher (48 mmol/mol or higher)
- Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG) (after an overnight fast of at least 8 hours):
  * Normal: Below 100 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L)
  * Impaired Fasting Glucose (Prediabetes): 100 to 125 mg/dL (5.6 to 6.9 mmol/L)
  * Diabetes: 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L) or higher
- 2-Hour Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT) (75-gram anhydrous glucose dissolved in water):
  * Normal: Below 140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L)
  * Impaired Glucose Tolerance (Prediabetes): 140 to 199 mg/dL (7.8 to 11.0 mmol/L)
  * Diabetes: 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) or higher
- Random Plasma Glucose: In a patient with classic symptoms of severe hyperglycemia or hyperglycemic crisis, a random plasma glucose >= 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) confirms diagnosis.

2. GLYCEMIC TARGETS FOR NON-PREGNANT ADULTS WITH DIABETES
- General Adult HbA1c Target: Less than 7.0% (53 mmol/mol) for most non-pregnant adults without significant hypoglycemia.
- Stringent Target (< 6.5%): May be appropriate for selected individuals with short disease duration, long life expectancy, and no significant cardiovascular disease, if achievable without hypoglycemia.
- Less Stringent Target (< 8.0%): Appropriate for patients with severe history of hypoglycemia, limited life expectancy, advanced microvascular or macrovascular complications, or extensive comorbid conditions.
- Preprandial Capillary Blood Glucose: 80 to 130 mg/dL (4.4 to 7.2 mmol/L).
- Peak Postprandial Capillary Blood Glucose (1-2 hours after beginning of meal): Less than 180 mg/dL (10.0 mmol/L).

3. FREQUENCY OF HBA1C TESTING
- Perform HbA1c test at least twice a year in patients who are meeting treatment goals and have stable glycemic control.
- Perform HbA1c quarterly (every 3 months) in patients whose therapy has changed or who are not meeting glycemic goals.
